# Introduction

This is a panoramic drawing of the city of the Blessed Name, a level from the video game Blasphmeous II in the style of The Game Kitchen studio.

<div class="text-center p-4">
  <img width="1920px" class="img-fluid" src="https://i.imgur.com/tvuM241.png">
</div>


## Initial concept

Before starting to draw, I had to research the inspiration behind The Game Kitchen so I could understand how to draw in a similar way. 
I knew that part of the inspiration for the Blasphemous series came from the architecture of Sevilla, so I researched some examples of churches in the city and also used some concepts and screenshots from the game as reference to better understand the architecture and its characteristics.

With some references at hand I drew the first concept of the drawing, which did not have much detail, but it helped me know what perspective and how many churches and towers I should draw approximately.

## Replicating the style

Una de las partes mas difíciles era mas que dibujar un gran numero de edificios, replicar con fidelidad la arqutiectura barroca sevillana, la cual se caracteriza, entre otras cosas, por tener una sobrecarga de detalle en sus edificaciones, lo que me obligaba a dibujar con detalle cada columna, tejado y fachada de cada edificio. 

<div class="text-center p-4">
  <img width="700px" class="img-fluid" src="https://i.imgur.com/Gu0c5w3.jpeg">
</div>

This screenshot was one of the most important references for making the drawing, since it helped me not only understand the use of color in the scene but also because it showed me the use of lights and shadows in more detail than some screenshots from the first game. However, unlike the first game, the buildings that appear in the background of the game do not appear in an isometric view, but rather frontally. Therefore, at this point, I had to understand exactly the parts of a church to know what to draw behind the towers that appeared in the game, or at least simulate it in a way that seemed believable.

<div class="text-center p-4">
  <img width="700px" class="img-fluid" src="https://i.imgur.com/v0LH21w.png">
</div>

Luckily, the game's sky was the easiest to replicate since it didn't require changing any elements or drawing them from other perspectives, as the current design also worked with my drawing. So I could focus on drawing the clouds in the same way as the original drawing, using the same colors that appear in the composition.


## Process and final version

Normally, once I have the concept of the drawing, the first thing I do is draw the silhouette of the different elements of the drawing with the same color, to later add the rest of the colors and details, however, due to the amount of elements that I had to draw this time I also drew the shadows first, to be able to not only save work in the future, but also to understand and differentiate the composition more easily as I added more elements.

<div class="text-center p-4">
  <img width="700px" class="img-fluid" src="https://i.imgur.com/IV3MTrk.gif">
</div>

Once I had all the buildings and the sky drawn, I moved on to adding various tones to the buildings to differentiate them based on their distance. Next, I added some colors and details to the building roofs and also to the penitent one. Finally, I added lights and shadows to give it volume and I corrected some minor details.
